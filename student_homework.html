How to Send Your Homework to Your Teacher on Preply

Follow these simple steps:

Step 1
📥
Download File
Click the pink "Download Homework (.txt)" button above to save your homework report onto your phone or computer.
Step 2
💬
Open Preply Chat
Log into Preply app or website and open the chat message window with your English teacher.
Step 3
📎
Attach Document
Click the paperclip or plus icon (📎) next to the message box and select your downloaded file (WouldYouRather_Homework.txt).
Step 4
🚀
Send & Celebrate!
Press Send! Your teacher will receive your full score breakdown and quiz answers.
